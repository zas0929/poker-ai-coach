# Chip sound sources

Both source pages list Creative Commons Zero (CC0).

- `chip-bet.wav`: first second of preview option 3, containing one chip drop. Source: **Poker Chip Drop**, Za-Games — https://freesound.org/people/Za-Games/sounds/540369/
- `pot-win.wav`: first second of preview option 4 (low-pass filtered and level-adjusted). Source: **Poker Chips Handling**, Flip_Book — https://freesound.org/people/Flip_Book/sounds/850378/

A short fade at the cut prevents an audible edge. Both are served locally, without runtime requests to Freesound.
